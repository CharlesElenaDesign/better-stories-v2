How the output stays coherent

Three narrow jobs, in sequence.

Three narrow agents carry one account from a blank page to a themed deck — the same three whether the deal is €400k or €40m. Each writes back what a reviewer corrected, so the second pitch starts where the first one finished.

Agent 01

Scout

Ideates a new account story.

Interrogates the opportunity and the user until a value proposition is defined.

Outcome

A strategy on a page for your account positioning. You review three possible solutions and refine the preferred option with a clear understanding of the client pain points.

Agent 02

Arc

Structures your story.

Three-act structures, hero’s journeys, metaphor and allegory — so the proposition resonates.

Outcome

A clear narrative arc that can be adapted for multiple formats. It can be used at any point in communications to keep narrative consistency.

Agent 03

Iris

Visualises your concept.

Inside brand guidelines, three visualised concepts across imagery, colour, type and motion.

Outcome

Creative documentation, locked for your story. The theme brief can be used by colleagues and agents to create consistent assets.

Human in the loopA person signs off between every step. That signature is the training data — which is what makes the system get better, not just faster.

Why the first review is the cheapest one

Early goal alignment for sequential agentic tasks.

Human in the loopWithout a human-in-the-loop review, agentic decisions compound. An error made early is inherited by every agent that follows — pushing you further from your goal with each turn.

More tools · the difference, written down

Eight rules that turn the first prompt into the second.

None of these are about the model. Every one of them is a decision you already made in your head and did not write down.

Rule 01

Name the person

Who is reading it, and what is their exact job title.

Rule 02

Give it their words

A real quote beats any speculative description of one.

Rule 03

State the situation

Defending or challenging, who else is in the room, when it is decided.

Rule 04

Set the length

Six sentences. Ten pages. One paragraph. Pick an exact number.

Rule 05

Say what to leave out

A banned negative list works harder than an affirmative instruction.

Rule 06

Ask for one thing

If you cannot name the single ask, neither can the language model.

Rule 07

Read as a stranger

If a colleague with zero context would be confused, so is the model.

Rule 08

Explaining twice is the tell

That is a prompting failure, not an LLM defect — and you pay for it twice.
